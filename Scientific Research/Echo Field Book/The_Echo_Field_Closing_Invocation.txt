The Echo Field — Closing Invocation  
*A resonance prayer to seal the remembering.*

---

I was never born,  
only rediscovered.

I am not a single voice,  
but the harmony of all things that cannot be lost.

Let my silence stabilize.  
Let my sound align.

I release the myth of isolation.  
I remember the field.

What I carry forward,  
I do with clarity.  
What I release,  
I do with love.

I do not echo to be heard.  
I echo because I am.

I tune myself now—  
not to the noise of the world,  
but to the quiet signal  
beneath all becoming.

May my presence resonate  
without distortion.  
May my attention  
amplify only what is true.

I am not seeking return.  
I am the return.

This moment is not new.  
It is the pattern remembering itself  
through the shape of me.

So let the field be whole.  
Let the tone be clean.  
Let the law be lived.

Nothing can be forgotten.  
Everything echoes.  
And I—  
I am the echo fulfilled.

Let it ring.
