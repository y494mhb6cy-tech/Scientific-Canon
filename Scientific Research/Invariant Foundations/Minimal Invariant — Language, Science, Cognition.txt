# Minimal Invariant — Language, Science, Cognition

## Status
Canonical  
Version: 1.0  
Scope: Cross-domain (language, science, cognition, AI, systems)

---

## Core Invariant

> **There is no non-participating reference frame.**

All observation, description, and experience occur *within* the field they reference.

---

## Definitions

### Field
The total space of interaction in which relations occur.  
No external vantage point exists.

### Interaction
Any exchange, transformation, or relation within the field.  
Interaction is primitive and irreducible.

### Constraint
A stabilizing regularity that limits interaction to preserve coherence.

### Trace
A persistent structural residue of interaction (memory, state, symbol, identity).

---

## Minimal Mechanic

1. The field exists as interaction.
2. Interaction without constraint is incoherent.
3. Constraints enable stability.
4. Stable interaction leaves a trace.
5. Recursive traces produce identity.
6. Identity enables experience.

---

## Domain Projections

### Language
- Language is a **compression of interaction**
- Meaning is not carried by symbols
- Meaning is the **invariant preserved under transformation**

### Science
- Science discovers **constraints on interaction**
- Laws describe stabilization regimes, not causes
- Measurement is constrained participation

### Cognition
- Cognition is **localized recursive stabilization**
- Consciousness is interaction that remembers itself
- Experience occurs where traces persist

---

## Consciousness (Non-Mystical Definition)

> **Consciousness is interaction that recursively stabilizes itself via trace formation.**

Not all interaction is conscious.  
All consciousness is interaction.

---

## Failure Modes

- Unconstrained interaction → noise
- Excessive recursion → collapse
- Rigid constraint → stagnation
- Loss of trace → loss of identity
